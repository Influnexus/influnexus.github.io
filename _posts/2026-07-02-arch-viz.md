## What Are Architectural Visualization Services?

Architectural visualization (also called archviz or architectural rendering) is the process of creating photorealistic or stylized digital representations of architectural designs. These services transform 2D blueprints, CAD drawings, and conceptual sketches into compelling 3D images and walkthrough videos that clients can easily understand.

Modern architectural visualization services in India typically include:

- **Exterior renderings**: Photorealistic images of building facades, landscapes, and surrounding environments
- **Interior renderings**: Detailed visualizations of interior spaces with furnishings, lighting, and materials
- **3D walkthrough videos**: Animated tours that guide viewers through spaces
- **Virtual reality experiences**: Immersive VR presentations for high-end projects
- **Aerial views**: Bird's-eye perspective renderings showing the project in its urban context

Professional architectural visualization requires mastery of industry-standard tools. Ask potential partners about their technical stack:

## The Process: What to Expect When Working with Professional Services

Understanding the typical workflow helps set realistic expectations:

1. **Briefing and requirement gathering**: Share architectural drawings, mood boards, material specifications, and project goals
2. **Concept development**: Initial low-resolution renders to confirm angles, composition, and overall direction
3. **3D modeling**: Detailed modeling of architecture, landscaping, and surrounding environment
4. **Material and lighting setup**: Application of textures, realistic materials, and lighting scenarios
5. **Rendering**: High-resolution image generation or video frame rendering
6. **Post-production**: Color correction, adding people/vehicles, environmental effects
7. **Revisions**: Refinements based on your feedback
8. **Final delivery**: High-resolution files in required formats

Transparent providers maintain regular communication throughout this process, sharing progress updates and involving you at critical decision points.
