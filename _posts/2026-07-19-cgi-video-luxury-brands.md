## Why Luxury & Fashion Brands Choose Influnexus

Luxury brands cannot compromise visual quality, but physical shoots are slow and expensive to repeat per SKU or per market. Photoreal CGI built from design files lets a luxury brand launch before physical stock exists, at the same visual bar as a shoot.

## Our Process

1. **Discovery call (30 min)** — brand, goals, timeline and budget scoped together
2. **Strategy & script (3–5 days)** — creative direction, moodboards, storyboards aligned before production
3. **Production (1–4 weeks)** — CGI workflows, AI generation and live production as the brief demands
4. **Delivery & launch** — mastered assets, platform-optimized cuts, launch support
