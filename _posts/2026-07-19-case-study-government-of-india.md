## Executive Summary

The Government of India needed a public-sector initiative translated into a video campaign that landed with citizens across 22 languages, at national broadcast scale. Influnexus produced a CGI-heavy hero video with regional voice-over masters and built multi-language motion templates that state teams used to adapt 18 regional cuts independently.

## Client

**Government of India** — national public-sector initiative.

## Industry

Government

## Business Challenge

A public-sector message has to reach citizens across radically different languages and regions without losing message accuracy or visual consistency. National broadcast requirements meant the campaign needed to work at scale, and state-level teams needed a way to localize the master without commissioning 22 separate productions.

## Creative Strategy

Build one CGI-heavy hero video as the visual and narrative master, then engineer the entire motion graphics layer as reusable templates — so voice-over, on-screen text and regional detail could be swapped per language without rebuilding the campaign from scratch for each of the 22 variants.

## Production Pipeline

- **Hero production:** CGI-heavy national hero video built to broadcast standard
- **Localization system:** Motion graphic templates engineered for language-swap without re-render of the full sequence
- **Voice-over masters:** Regional VO recorded and synced across all 22 language variants
- **Rollout enablement:** Templates handed to state teams for independent regional adaptation

## Deliverables

- 1 national hero video (CGI)
- 22 language variants
- 18 state-level regional cuts (adapted by state teams from the delivered templates)
- Broadcast + digital masters

## Technology Stack

CGI production pipeline · multi-language motion graphic templating

## Results

- **22** language variants delivered
- Aired across **national broadcast + digital**
- State teams independently adapted the master into **18 regional cuts** using the delivered motion templates

## Why This Matters

Government and public-sector campaigns fail when localization is treated as an afterthought — 22 separate productions would have blown both timeline and budget. Engineering the motion system for language-swap from day one let state teams self-serve 18 regional cuts without commissioning Influnexus for each one. For any organization running a message across multiple languages or regions, this templated-localization model is the difference between one campaign and 22.
