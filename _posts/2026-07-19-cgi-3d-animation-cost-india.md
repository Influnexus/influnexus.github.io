## What Actually Drives CGI Cost Up or Down

Price is not arbitrary. Six factors move it more than anything else:

**1. Photorealism level.** Stylized 3D is cheaper than photoreal. A photoreal chrome-and-glass product with accurate refraction, subsurface scattering and studio lighting takes far more render and artist time than a flat stylized render.

**2. Modeling complexity.** A simple bottle is a day. A watch movement, a car interior, or a machine with hundreds of parts is weeks. If you have CAD files, modeling cost drops sharply — always ask.

**3. Animation vs stills.** A still is one frame. Animation is 24–30 frames per second, each rendered and art-directed. Motion multiplies cost, which is why duration and frame rate matter to the quote.

**4. Revisions.** Most overruns come from vague briefs and endless revision rounds. Studios that quote low often cap revisions tightly. A clear brief up front is the single biggest cost-saver.

**5. Render time & resolution.** 4K, high sample counts, and complex simulations (fluids, cloth, smoke) burn render-farm hours. Social-first 1080p is cheaper than broadcast 4K.

**6. Studio tier.** Freelancers are cheapest but carry delivery risk. Boutique studios balance quality and cost. Large post houses charge premium overhead. For most brand work, a specialist boutique is the value sweet spot.

## How to Budget Your Project (Without Overpaying)

- **Lead with the deliverable, not the medium.** "One 20-second photoreal product film for Instagram, 1080p, 2 revision rounds" gets an accurate quote fast. "Some CGI stuff" does not.
- **Share reference frames.** Three reference images tell a studio more than three pages of brief. They anchor the quality tier and the price.
- **Bundle.** Commissioning stills + a film + social cutdowns together is far cheaper per asset than three separate briefs.
- **Provide assets.** CAD models, brand guidelines, and existing product photography cut modeling and setup hours.
- **Fix scope before you start.** Lock deliverables, resolution, and revision count in writing. Scope creep is the #1 budget killer.

## FAQ

### How much does a CGI product video cost in India?
A short CGI product film (15–30 seconds, photoreal, ad-grade) typically costs between ₹1,50,000 and ₹8,00,000 in India in 2026. The final price depends on modeling complexity, photorealism level, animation length, resolution, and the number of revision rounds. Simpler stylized work sits at the lower end; full photoreal hero films with complex materials sit at the top.

### Is 3D animation cheaper in India than in Dubai?
Yes. For the same deliverable, Dubai and UAE studios typically charge 1.8 to 3 times more than Indian studios, mainly due to higher operating and labor costs. This is why many Gulf and international brands commission Indian studios for CGI and 3D animation — the quality is comparable while the cost is significantly lower.

### What is the cost of architectural 3D visualization in India?
A single architectural visualization still in India costs roughly ₹15,000 to ₹75,000 per view in 2026. A full animated walkthrough of 60 to 90 seconds ranges from ₹2,50,000 to ₹12,00,000, depending on interior detail, lighting realism, resolution, and duration.

### How can I reduce my CGI production cost?
The biggest cost savings come from a clear brief, sharing reference frames, providing CAD files or existing product photography, bundling multiple deliverables into one commission, and locking scope and revision count in writing before production starts. Scope creep and vague briefs are the most common causes of budget overruns.

### How long does a CGI or 3D animation project take?
A single CGI product still typically takes 2 to 5 working days. A short photoreal product film of 15 to 30 seconds usually takes 2 to 5 weeks from approved brief to final delivery, depending on modeling complexity, animation length, and revision rounds.
