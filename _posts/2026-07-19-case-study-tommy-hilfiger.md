## Executive Summary

Tommy Hilfiger needed to launch a Spring/Summer drop in 6 weeks with no physical product available for a shoot — only design files. Influnexus built a Houdini-to-Unreal CGI pipeline that placed 14 SKUs into 9 photoreal lifestyle environments, delivering 9 hero videos and 36 social cutdowns in 5 weeks. Campaign ran across Instagram, YouTube and DOOH in India and the GCC.

## Client

**Tommy Hilfiger** — global fashion house, Spring/Summer campaign, India + GCC markets.

## Industry

Luxury / Fashion

## Business Challenge

A hard launch date with no product in hand. Standard fashion production — studio shoot, location shoot, model casting — was not an option inside the 6-week window, and design files were the only asset available. Any CGI solution had to hold up at the same visual standard as a physical shoot, across 14 different SKUs, with zero room for a reshoot.

## Creative Strategy

Rather than treat CGI as a fallback, Influnexus treated it as the primary production method: build 9 distinct photoreal lifestyle environments that could carry the brand's premium positioning, then populate them with product built directly from design files — no physical reference needed.

## Production Pipeline

- **Modeling & materials:** Product geometry and material shaders built from Tommy Hilfiger design files in Cinema 4D
- **Environment build:** 9 photoreal lifestyle environments constructed in Houdini
- **Render pipeline:** Houdini-to-Unreal workflow for real-time photoreal lighting and render throughput
- **Compositing:** AI-assisted compositing layered CGI product into environments
- **Cutdown production:** Hero films re-cut into 36 platform-specific social variants

## Deliverables

- 9 hero campaign videos
- 36 social cutdowns (Instagram, YouTube formats)
- DOOH-ready master files
- 14 SKUs across 9 environments

## Technology Stack

Houdini · Cinema 4D · Unreal Engine · AI-assisted compositing

## Results

- **5 weeks** concept to delivery
- **9** hero videos + **36** social cutdowns shipped
- Campaign carried the launch across IG, YouTube and DOOH in **India + GCC**

## Why This Matters

For luxury and fashion brands, CGI removes the hardest constraint in a launch calendar — physical product availability — without compromising the visual standard the category demands. A campaign that would normally require a multi-location shoot, model casting and weeks of logistics was compressed into a 5-week pipeline running entirely from design files. For any brand launching before physical stock exists, this is the production model.
