## Red Flags to Watch When Selecting a CGI Studio

Not every company claiming to be the best CGI company in Mumbai will meet your standards. Watch for these warning signs:

**Generic Portfolio**: If every project looks similar or samples appear outdated, the studio may lack creative range or current technical capabilities. Demand recent work relevant to your industry.

**Unclear Pricing Structure**: Reputable studios provide detailed quotes breaking down modeling, texturing, rendering, revisions, and additional costs. Vague estimates often lead to budget overruns.

**Poor Communication During Inquiry**: If responsiveness is lacking before a contract is signed, expect worse communication during production. Test their response time and clarity during the evaluation phase.

**No Revision Policy**: CGI projects require iteration. Studios should clearly outline how many revision rounds are included and costs for additional changes. Lack of this policy suggests potential disputes ahead.

**Absence of Client Testimonials**: Established studios serving markets from Delhi to Dubai should have verifiable client references, case studies, or testimonials. Anonymous portfolio pieces without context raise questions about legitimacy.

## Questions to Ask Before Hiring Your CGI Partner

Make your selection process more effective by asking potential studios these critical questions:

1. **Can you show examples of work for clients in Dubai or the broader Middle East?** This reveals cultural awareness and experience with Gulf market standards.

2. **What is your typical project timeline from brief to final delivery?** Understanding realistic timelines prevents missed launch dates.

3. **How do you handle revisions and feedback loops?** Clarify processes before misunderstandings arise.

4. **What file formats and resolutions will you deliver?** Ensure outputs match your requirements for various platforms—social media, broadcast, print, or digital billboards.

5. **Can you provide references from similar projects?** Speaking with previous clients offers insights beyond what portfolios reveal.

6. **What rendering technology do you use?** Studios using current rendering engines (V-Ray, Arnold, Octane) produce superior results faster than those relying on outdated technology.
