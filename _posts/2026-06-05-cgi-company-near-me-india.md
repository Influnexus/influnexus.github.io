## Evaluating Technical Capabilities and Portfolio Quality

Technical infrastructure separates amateur operations from professional studios. When researching CGI companies serving the Dubai market, investigate their technology stack and production pipeline.

**Software and Tools**: Industry-standard applications include Autodesk Maya, 3ds Max, Cinema 4D for modeling; V-Ray, Arnold, or Octane for rendering; and Houdini for complex simulations. ## Pricing Models and Contract Considerations

Understanding how CGI companies structure their pricing helps you budget appropriately and avoid hidden costs. When comparing quotes from different India-based studios, look beyond the bottom line.

**Project-Based vs. Hourly Rates**: Most CGI work is quoted per project, with pricing determined by complexity, number of assets, and delivery timeline. Simple product renders might start at $500-1000 per image, while complex architectural visualizations can range from $3000-15000 depending on detail level and revisions required.

**Retainer Arrangements**: Businesses with ongoing content needs often benefit from monthly retainers. This model provides predictable costs and priority scheduling, particularly valuable for Dubai agencies managing multiple client campaigns simultaneously.

**Scope Creep Protection**: Well-written contracts clearly define deliverables, revision limits, and additional fees for scope changes. This protects both parties and sets realistic expectations from day one.

Indian studios serving international clients typically accept multiple payment methods including wire transfers, PayPal, and sometimes cryptocurrency. Payment terms vary but often follow a 50% upfront, 50% upon delivery structure for new clients.

## Quality Assurance and Delivery Standards

Before committing to any CGI company, establish clear quality benchmarks and delivery expectations. Professional studios should willingly discuss their quality assurance processes.

**Technical Specifications**: Define required resolution, file formats, color profiles, and aspect ratios upfront. For content destined for Dubai's numerous high-resolution displays and premium print publications, 4K rendering and 16-bit color depth might be necessary.
